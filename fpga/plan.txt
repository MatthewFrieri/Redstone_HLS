FPGA v1 Architecture (2x3 CLBs)

3 bit routing channels
disjoint switch boxes, signals stay on the same tracks. requires 6 ROM bits per track
connection boxes for inputs/outputs of CLBs. require 1 ROM bit per track per input/output
CLB has 2 inputs and 1 output, contains 1 LUT, requires 4 ROM bits


PLACING METHODOLOGY

describes how to generate the bitstream to load one boolean expression.
the expression should have one bit output Y, and should use up to 3 one bit inputs A B C.

examples:  
    Y = A or B
    Y = (not B) or (A and C)
    Y = not (not B)
    Y = not ((not B) or (A and C))


the v1 FPGA architecture only supports 3 inputs and 1 output, so it is not ambiguous where they should be placed.
a caveat with this architecture is that each binary operation requires its own CLB, even a NOT operation


IMPLEMENTATION

generate a binary tree of operations for the input boolean expression. the root of the tree is Y and the leaves are A B C.
generate an undirected graph G of the empty fpga board. use nested objects?

starting at the max level of the tree, place the id of the node at the closest remaing CLB in G.
attempt to wire the children of the placed node to its own inputs. use BFS for this?
will have to lookup the children from their ids. have some structure to map ids to location on graph.
this is valid because lower level nodes are always placed after their higher level children.
